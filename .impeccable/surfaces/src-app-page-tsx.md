Mode: Persuade. Audience: the default-caregiver sibling (buyer) and the hackathon jury (EN/RU/KZ). Action: "Try Sarah's family" (working demo, no sign-up); secondary "Start your own". Proof: the demo family's real month computed by lib/money from the seed. Offer: siblings free, caregiver $5.99/mo, first month free. ## Direction contract

THESIS: The scattered evidence of care already lives on the family fridge; Kinvoice turns that door into one fair invoice. Refuses the split hero of headline-left, abstract card-right and the row of three equal "how it works" cards.

OWN-WORLD: Deep cobalt appliance-enamel field with a soft vertical sheen covering the whole first viewport; real paper objects on it: thermal receipt (cool white, faded ink, mono), manila appointment card with a red rule, canary sticky note in handwriting, crisp white invoice sheet. Round enamel magnets (tomato, marigold, mint) hold every object. Small printed white labels with hairline pointers name what Kinvoice reads from each scrap. Type: Unbounded display, Golos Text body, Caveat only for handwriting, JetBrains Mono only on receipts. All have Kazakh Cyrillic.

STORY: Visitor recognises her own fridge (the problem: costs and hours only she sees), sees the invoice Mike opens with $218.40 and a pay button (the product), reads the price, clicks Try Sarah's family.
